The Reality

Nigeria runs on backup power.

For most Nigerian homes and businesses, the grid is a supplement, not a guarantee. Diesel and petrol generators fill the gap — noisy, expensive to fuel, and hard on the equipment that depends on them.

Solar and battery systems solve that problem, but only when they are sized correctly. An undersized system still leaves you running the generator, and an oversized one costs far more than it needs to.
